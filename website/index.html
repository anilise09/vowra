NEW IN THE APP

Built in,
not bolted on.

See why you might click.

Discover puts people who share your interests and goals first, and says what you have in common. It never ranks anyone by popularity.

Meet who you’re looking for.

Say how you identify and who you’d like to meet. Two people only see each other when each fits the other’s choice, and your choice is never shown to anyone.

Your data, yours to take.

Download a copy of everything Vawra keeps about you, from Settings. It holds your own messages, never anyone else’s.

Call when you’re both ready.

Voice and video calls unlock only when both of you turn on “Open to a call” for that match. Calls are never recorded, and you can report or block without leaving the call.

A heads-up when it matters.

If a match asks for money or gift cards, pushes you to another app or sends a link, you get a quiet warning and a Report button. Only you see it, and nothing is blocked for you.

Distance, not your location.

Distance is optional. Your phone rounds its location to about 2 km before sending it, others only ever see a band like “5–10 km away”, and near places you mark private your distance is hidden.

YOUR SPACE, YOUR RULES

Feel good about
being here.

Good connections need room to grow—and clear boundaries. Vawra is building safety tools into the experience, not adding them as an afterthought.

  • 01 Block and report are always free, and blocking ends contact at once, calls included.
  • 02 Every photo is checked by a person before anyone else sees it.
  • 03 Photos in a chat arrive only if you allow them from that person, and stay blurred until you tap.
  • 04 Calls need a yes from both people and are never recorded.
  • 05 Reports reach a moderator; a suspended member can appeal to a different one.

GOOD TO KNOW

Some straight
answers.

Can I sign up today?

Not yet. Vawra is in development. The screens here come from the app in testing, with synthetic sample profiles; there is no public dating service or waitlist on this site.

Are the people shown real members?

No. Every profile shown on this website is a synthetic test profile. We do not use fake profiles or engagement in a live service.

Will messaging and safety tools cost extra?

No. Matching, messaging, blocking, reporting, and core safety controls are intended to remain free.

How do calls work?

Inside a chat, each of you can turn on “Open to a call”. Only when both have can either of you start a voice or video call. The camera and microphone are asked for when a call starts, calls are never recorded, and blocking ends a call at once.

Does Vawra know where I am?

Only if you turn distance on. Your phone then sends an area about 2 km across, never your exact spot, and other people see only a band such as “10–25 km away”. You can mark private places, like home, where your distance is hidden; those are kept only on your phone.

Who sees my photos?

Profile photos are checked by a person before anyone else sees them, and their location data is removed. Photos in a chat need the other person to allow photos from you, are checked the same way, and arrive blurred until they tap.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Settings. Your account is removed after a 7-day grace period in case you change your mind, and you can download a copy of your data first. More about your data →

Is Vawra available on iPhone?

Vawra is being built with Flutter for Android and iPhone. iOS testing and release checks are still pending; this is not an App Store availability claim.
