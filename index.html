YOUR SPACE, YOUR RULES

Feel good about
being here.

Good connections need room to grow—and clear boundaries. Vawra is building safety tools into the experience, not adding them as an afterthought.

  • 01 Block and report are always free.
  • 02 No exact location shown on profiles.
  • 03 Calls require a match and explicit acceptance.

These are product design commitments. The service is not launched, and safety review remains in progress.

GOOD TO KNOW

Some straight
answers.

Can I sign up today?

Not yet. Vawra is in development. The screens here show an offline prototype and synthetic sample profiles; there is no public dating service or waitlist on this site.

Are the people shown real members?

No. Every profile shown on this website is a synthetic test profile. We do not use fake profiles or engagement in a live service.

Will messaging and safety tools cost extra?

No. Matching, messaging, blocking, reporting, and core safety controls are intended to remain free.

Is Vawra available on iPhone?

Vawra is being built with Flutter for Android and iPhone. iOS testing and release checks are still pending; this is not an App Store availability claim.
