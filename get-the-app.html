Vawra is an 18+ dating app in development. The Android prototype is ready to try: it shows how Vawra works with synthetic test profiles, needs no account and sends nothing anywhere. The iPhone version follows once Apple’s TestFlight is set up. Below are the steps for each phone and a short recording of the real app being installed.

Android

Prototype

Needs Android 7.0 or later on a 64-bit phone (almost every phone from 2017 on). 80 MB.

Android app prototype
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the Android button. Your browser saves the Vawra app file.
  2. Open the file. Android asks before it installs an app that does not come from an app store. If it does, allow your browser: Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps, choose your browser, and turn on Allow. The names differ a little between phones.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the community guidelines.
  5. Create your profile: your name, your age, who you would like to meet, what you are looking for and a few interests. Photos, habits and prompts can wait.

Once Vawra is installed, you can turn Install unknown apps off again for your browser. Only install Vawra from this page.

iPhone

Not available yet

Needs iOS 15 or later.

iPhone app not available yet
  1. Install TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying apps before they launch, from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. Open your Vawra invitation on the iPhone. TestFlight opens it.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.
  4. Open Vawra, confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the community guidelines.
  5. Create your profile, the same way as on Android.

Before launch, Apple allows iPhone apps to be shared only through TestFlight invitations. TestFlight keeps the test version up to date.

Questions

What is in the Android prototype?

The whole Vawra experience with synthetic test profiles: onboarding, Discover, matches, chat with suggested openers, safety tools and settings. It works without an account, stores your answers only on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. Signing in, real members and calls come later, after age checks, moderation and an independent security review are in place.

Why is the iPhone version not available yet?

Before launch, Apple allows apps to be shared only through TestFlight invitations, and TestFlight is not set up for Vawra yet. When it is, the iPhone button on this page switches on.

Is it a real dating service?

Not yet. Vawra is not a live dating service, and the profiles in the prototype and the recording are synthetic test profiles.

How do I remove it?

On Android, touch and hold the Vawra icon and choose Uninstall. On iPhone, touch and hold the icon and choose Remove App. Deleting your account is separate: it is in the app under Settings.
