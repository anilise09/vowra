# Competitive research — 2026-09-20

Pricing varies by country, platform, promotion, and experiment. Final prices must come from the user's app store at purchase time.

| Product | Free relationship loop | Paid model | Calling / trust pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tinder | Match and chat, limited discovery | Plus adds unlimited likes, rewinds, travel/incognito; Gold adds Likes You/visibility inventory; Platinum adds priority | Monetization centers on discovery and visibility |
| Bumble | Swipe, match, message | Boost adds volume/backtrack/extensions; Premium adds Likes You, filters, incognito/travel; Premium+ adds ranking/insights; boosts are consumable | Free in-app voice/video after matching; photo/ID verification, explicit-image blur, scam detection, Share Date |
| Hinge | Match and message with limited likes | Hinge+ adds unlimited likes, Likes You, preferences; HingeX adds recommendations and priority; Roses/Boosts are consumable | Profile prompts and recommendation quality differentiate it |
| Pure | Time-sensitive likes/chats | Weekly/monthly/yearly access plus feature inventory | Match chat has voice/video, expiring media/chat, screenshot deterrence, incognito |

## Conclusions

1. Keep the connection loop intact and charge for efficiency: discovery volume, advanced filters, travel/incognito, and optional visibility.
2. Video calling reduces phone-number sharing and catfishing risk, so it should be free, match-gated, accept/decline, block-aware, and unrecorded.
3. Launch needs moderation operations—not merely a report button: policies, detection, triage, appeals, evidence retention, and response targets.
4. Do not copy competitor prices. Start with transparent regional tiers and no personalized price discrimination.
5. Never promise media is impossible to capture; a second device can always record it.

## Proposed differentiation

**Consent before contact, context before ranking.** Relationship intent and communication comfort are explicit. Text opens at match; calls unlock only after both people opt in for that match. Profiles use prompts and an optional verified intro clip. Discovery explains compatibility reasons without claiming an AI soulmate score.

- Mutual call readiness rather than surprise ringing
- Date Check-in shared with a trusted contact
- Graceful Close to end a chat without ghosting or revealing reports
- Privacy zones and coarse distance bands
- Safety center on every chat and call screen
