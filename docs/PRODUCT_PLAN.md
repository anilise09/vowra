# Product plan

## Positioning

An inclusive 18+ app for intentional casual or long-term connections without surrendering privacy. Relationship intent is explicit, never inferred from behavior.

## MVP journeys

1. Account, age gate, community-rule consent, privacy choices.
2. Profile with photos, prompts, intent, identity, and coarse location.
3. Discovery using mutual eligibility and preferences.
4. Like/pass; a mutual like creates exactly one match.
5. Real-time messaging with image safety and anti-spam limits.
6. Mutual call readiness followed by audio/video call requests.
7. Block, unmatch, and report from profile/chat/call.
8. Pause, delete, and export personal data.

## Monetization hypothesis

Free includes profile, reasonable discovery, matching, messaging, audio/video, and all safety controls.

**Plus experiment:** USD 14.99/month or USD 89.99/year before localization: unlimited likes, rewind, advanced preferences, incognito, travel.

**Select experiment:** USD 24.99/month or USD 149.99/year: Plus, Likes You, weekly priority introductions, a monthly boost, explainable enhanced recommendations.

Consumables may include boosts and priority introductions. Never sell messages, reports, verification, or video safety. StoreKit/Play Billing supply localized prices; server entitlements come only from verified store transactions.

## Metrics

- Reciprocal conversation and healthy seven-day conversation rates
- Call opt-in and successful-connection rates
- Report rate per active conversation, block latency, moderation response time
- Appeal overturn rate
- Conversion, renewal, refund, and cancellation completion

Do not optimize compulsive swipe time or raw message count at the expense of safety or match quality.
